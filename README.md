## 📋 Research Overview

This research investigates cyber harassment and stalking in the Maldives, with a specific focus on the legislative gaps that persist even after the December 17, 2025 Penal Code amendments. Despite new cybercrime legislation aligning with the Budapest Convention, harassment and stalking remain unaddressed in Maldivian law.

### Research Question
**"How does the absence of specific cyber harassment and stalking legislation in Maldives impact vulnerable populations, particularly journalists and female politicians?"**

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- **173 cybercrime cases** reported in 2024 (↑74.7% from 2023)
- **25% of female politicians** shut down social media accounts due to harassment
- **60% of female journalists** experience online violence
- **$2.2 million** lost to financial scams in 2022 alone
- **Zero specific laws** addressing cyber harassment or stalking as of December 2025

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

### Primary Data (`primary_data_harassment_cases.csv`)
- **30 documented cases** of cyber harassment (2019-2025)
- Sources: News reports, human rights organizations, parliamentary inquiries
- Categories: Death threats, doxxing, physical assaults, smear campaigns, etc.

### Secondary Data (`secondary_data_statistics.csv`)
- Police statistics from Maldives Police Service
- Westminster Foundation for Democracy study data
- Bank of Maldives financial crime statistics
- Media Bill voting records

### Legislative Comparison (`legislative_comparison.csv`)
- Comparison of cyber laws across 6 South Asian countries
- Analysis of legal gaps in Maldives

## 🔬 Methodology

### Data Collection
1. **Primary Research:** Manual collection of publicly documented harassment cases from:
   - International media reports
   - Human rights organization reports
   - Parliamentary inquiry data
   - Police announcements

2. **Secondary Research:** Compilation of official statistics from:
   - Maldives Police Service
   - Bank of Maldives
   - Westminster Foundation for Democracy
   - International Federation of Journalists

### Data Analysis
- Quantitative analysis of harassment types, targets, and trends
- Comparative legislative analysis with regional countries
- Temporal analysis showing escalation in 2024-2025

### Ethical Considerations
- No personal identifiable information beyond publicly reported names
- Focus on documented, public cases only
- Victim-centered approach avoiding sensationalization
- Compliance with UWE Bristol research ethics guidelines
